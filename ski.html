Kicker Run

One run down the mountain: 6,000 m, around three to five minutes from drop-in to the finish arch. The piste bends, steepens and flattens, with kickers, hips, rails, giant cliffs and woods trails along the way. Throw tricks, land them clean and chain your multiplier.

← → or A D
Carve on snow: hold a turn and the edges bite, tighter and faster. Nudge in the air
Space
Tap to jump anywhere. Hold in the air to grab
↑ W · ↓ S
Front flip / back flip in the air. ↓ tucks for speed on snow
Q E
Spin left / right. Flip and spin together for a cork bonus
Shift
Hold to speed up
J
Hold to slow down
F
Hold in the air for slow motion. Clean tricks refill it
