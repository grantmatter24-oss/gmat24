Loop Pad

Sounds are synthesized in the browser in the style of Kettama's rave-house (Fly Away XTC) and UK breaks / garage — not samples from their records. Keys: 1-4, Q-R, A-F, Z-V play pads · Space = stop (ends recording) · P = play loop · Enter = record. Hits snap to 16th notes. Pads marked “hold me” sustain while held.
