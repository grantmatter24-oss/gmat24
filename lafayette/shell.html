W A S D or arrow keys: throttle, brake, steer (hold brake at a stop to reverse the car or quad). Space: rear brake or handbrake. Shift / Ctrl: lean back / forward for wheelies and in the air. R: back on your wheels. C: rider's-eye view. M: map, click to go there. T: time of day. H: lights. Drag the mouse to look around, wheel to zoom. Ride through a red arch to start its timed route.
Elevation: USGS 3DEP lidar. Streets, buildings, water: © OpenStreetMap contributors, via Overture Maps. Ground colour and tree cover: Copernicus Sentinel-2.
