Village
The shops on the cobbled plaza are real rooms: ski or walk in through the open door. In Parallel Mountain Sports (nearest the lifts), The Ski Wall (designer wide skis), Summit Accessories (packs, bandanas, helmet cameras, hats and helmets) and The Goggle Shop (nearest the Base to Base) press E to browse, pick things up, then pay at the counter with trick points or run out holding Shift: the owner chases you for ten seconds, and if you are caught it goes back. What you get goes in your locker (Outfit) Every restaurant, cafe and bar has a menu at the counter (E): order, sit at a table with E, and E eats or drinks it. The Wax Room hot waxes your skis for 2,000 pts: faster for three lift rides. On the plaza a band plays on the stage (E in front of it dances, E again changes the move), a stand pours hot chocolate, and the benches round the fires are seats. In The Auld Dubliner order as many pints as you like at the bar (E each), press E by a chair to sit, then E drinks one from the stein. Every second pint the room sways for three seconds.
